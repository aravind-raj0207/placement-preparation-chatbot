COMPANY: TCS
DATASET VERSION: 2026
TARGET: Freshers / campus-style hiring
SOURCE TYPE: Official company information
IMPORTANT: Hiring patterns can change by drive, role, batch and location.

CURRENT OFFICIAL PROCESS (TCS ALL INDIA NQT 2026)
- TCS NQT is an integrated test with Foundation and Advanced sections.
- Foundation: Numerical Ability (25 min), Verbal Ability (25 min), Reasoning Ability (25 min).
- Advanced: Advanced Quantitative & Reasoning Ability (25 min), Advanced Coding (90 min).
- Total test duration: 190 minutes.
- Advanced section is mandatory for candidates aspiring to Digital or Prime offers.
- Based on test performance, candidates can qualify for Prime, Digital or Ninja interviews.
- The 2026 TCS All India NQT page lists MCA among eligible qualifications and states a 60% minimum aggregate requirement across specified academic stages for that drive.
- The 2026 page states no pending backlog at the time of the selection process.

PREPARATION TOPICS
- Quantitative aptitude: arithmetic, percentages, ratios, averages, time/work, data interpretation.
- Reasoning: logical reasoning, patterns, arrangements and problem solving.
- Verbal: grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension.
- Coding: arrays, strings, searching, sorting, basic data structures, problem solving and implementation.
- Interview preparation: programming fundamentals, OOP, DBMS, SQL, OS, computer networks and project explanation.

QUESTION BANK POLICY
- Do NOT label specific interview questions as guaranteed TCS questions.
- Candidate-reported questions should be stored separately with year, source and confidence.
- Recommended chatbot wording: "This type of question has been reported in previous candidate experiences; the current interview may differ."

SAMPLE PREPARATION QUESTIONS
1. Explain OOP pillars with examples.
2. Difference between WHERE and HAVING in SQL.
3. Explain INNER JOIN vs LEFT JOIN.
4. What is normalization?
5. Explain process vs thread.
6. What is an IP address?
7. Reverse a string / array.
8. Find duplicates in an array.
9. Explain your final-year project.
10. Why do you want to work in IT services?

OFFICIAL SOURCE
https://www.tcs.com/careers/india/tcs-all-india-nqt-hiring

COMPANY: ACCENTURE
DATASET VERSION: 2026
TARGET: Graduate / entry-level roles
SOURCE TYPE: Official company information
IMPORTANT: Accenture explicitly states that the number of interviews and assessments depends on the role.

CURRENT OFFICIAL RECRUITMENT INFORMATION
- After application, Accenture reviews the application and may contact selected candidates for interviews.
- Interviews may be by phone, video or in person.
- The number of interviews depends on the role.
- Some roles include an online activity/technical assessment.
- Accenture says technical assessments evaluate problem solving, logical reasoning and applied technical knowledge.
- Coding environments may be used for engineering-oriented assessments.
- Accenture states that assessments are completed online in one sitting; typical assessment duration is approximately 30-90 minutes depending on proficiency and role.
- Assessment results are considered alongside the candidate's background and experience.
- Technical assessments do not replace interviews.

PREPARATION TOPICS
- Problem solving.
- Logical reasoning.
- Programming fundamentals.
- Coding and debugging.
- Role-specific technical knowledge.
- Communication and behavioral interview preparation.
- Resume and project explanation.

SAMPLE PREPARATION QUESTIONS
1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Explain your strongest technical skill.
3. Explain a project you built and your contribution.
4. Write a program to find duplicates in an array.
5. Explain time complexity.
6. What is the difference between a process and a thread?
7. Explain SQL joins.
8. Explain OOP concepts.
9. How would you debug a program that gives the wrong output?
10. Describe a difficult problem you solved.

CHATBOT NOTE
